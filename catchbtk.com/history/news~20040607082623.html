A message from author/criminalist Michael D. Kelleher to BTK:

You've nearly come full circle. So many years in the background, such a long time. Your self-control kept you alive and free, which is no small feat. Now, though, it's all beginning to wind down, isn't it? You understand change, don't you? It can be such a difficult, lonely stretch of road. It's not a happy place to walk alone.

We all face this time of life, the days when the end of the race is much closer than the beginning. It's a time of trial, a time when things need to be set right, put in order, made more clear to ourselves and those we love. It's a time when death is moving closer each day, so close that it is no longer a vision but a reality. These are the days when self-control is meaningless, a time when we must give ourselves over to what we know is inevitable. Still, it is as it should be. You are one of the lucky ones. You have made your race a long one, although it wasn't such a happy one. There are others you've touched who have been denied what you have now. Their race was cut short, their lives ended without a chance of knowing and experiencing what you have now. You know that there are reasons for this, but we wonder why it had to be so. Only you know. We are left to wonder.

Your photographs tell the story, but they have no dimension, no life. You have taken that away from them and, now, you have little left of them. Still, there is so much to tell, so much for us to understand, so much for you to organize and set right before you also die. This would be a fine time to take back your life in a positive, gentle way and not the way of your younger years. This would be the right time to open your heart and become the fullness you have so desperately wanted for so long.

kelleher@catchbtk.com
