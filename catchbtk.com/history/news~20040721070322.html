June 21, 2004
Son Of BTK Witness Speaks
By Tom Voigt
Webmaster
catchBTK.com

In December 1977, a fireman witnessed BTK making a phone call after murdering Nancy Fox. The witness' son recently agreed to this exclusive interview with catchBTK.com:

"I talked to him about the BTK case a couple of weeks ago, the first time since the incident happened. He told me the police had been in touch with him recently and asked for a DNA sample; he said they swabbed the inside of his mouth. He said it was to eliminate any posibility of him being a suspect. They said that they may ask for fingerprints later since he touched the phone receiver BTK made a call from.

"Here is the story he told me: He was working at a part-time job as most firefighters do. He was supposed to pick up a vehicle for the job but someone had the information wrong and the vehicle wasn't at the location it was suppose to be at. So, he decided to make a phone call to verify the location of the vehicle he was to pick up. He pulled into a grocery store parking lot and went inside to get change to make the phone call. He did notice that someone was using the pay phone as he entered the store.

"When he came out, he picked up the receiver to make a call and the dispatcher was on the phone and asked if he had just made a call. He said that he did not and that someone else was using the phone before him. The dispatcher recognized his voice and asked him if he could give a description. He said that he would try. (He told me that he had known the dispatcher because all emergency calls at that time were tied into the fire department. She recognized his voice because they had worked together for years.)

"He met later with the police who asked if they could try to hypnotize him and he agreed to that. He told me that it was a police officer that tried to hypnotize him and he didn't feel that they were successful in getting him under. I don't think that dad was paying too much attention to who was on the phone since he was trying to figure out where the vehicle he was to pick up was located.

"He did say that the person was about his own age or a little younger; my dad would have been in his late 40s or early 50s at the time. He said he thought the person had light brown hair and was wearing a work uniform similar to those worn by Kansas Gas Electric employees back then. I think they were light grey in color. That would be a good way for BTK to be somewhat invisible, being a meter reader.

"I hope they catch this guy, it's been too long and now that he is showing up and is back in contact I feel he will slip up."
