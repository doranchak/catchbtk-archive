BTK MAIL
This page contains actual e-mail received by catchBTK.com. Names and e-mail addresses have been deleted for privacy reasons. My comments are in italics.

Subject: Swabathon
Just a note to tell you how far the police are from catching this guy. A lady who works for me in Wichita said the Wichita police drove into the yard of her parents (they live in Mulvane, a small town south of Wichita) and asked her father to come outside. They told him he had been turned in as BTK! They swabbed him in his own front yard and then told him they had swabbed more than 3,000 people.
In that situation, I'm not sure what would have made me angrier; being suspected of being a serial killer, or having people drive on my lawn.
Subject: Carol Mould
A relative of Carol Mould was questioned as a BTK suspect years ago. When Carol was recently found murdered in her home in Benton, her purse was missing from the house. It was recovered, but her driver's license is still missing. Interesting that Wichita PD's main BTK guy, Lt. Landwehr, was at the scene about an hour after she was discovered. Benton is a small town just outside of Wichita and news travels fast in that town.
I've heard from several sources that someone close to Mould was considered a BTK suspect long before her death.
Subject: Hooters
An individual named John always goes to Hooters restaurant and never says a word, until one day he was reading a copy of the Wichita Eagle that had an article about BTK. One of the employees asked him what he thought about it and John flipped out saying they better not try to pin this on him and all kinds of other stuff and got up and left. Also, he is about 56 and lives near the area of the crime scenes. His answering machine also is weird; the voice resembles the BTK recording to 911.
The guy was in Hooters but was looking at a newspaper? That is weird!
Subject: Wichita Library
I met a librarian who works at the public library in Wichita where BTK left his last contact. She said that he left an envelope in the drop box and a bag outside. In it were old polaroids of victims. She would not tell me if they were victims which we already knew about or not. Also was a polaroid of the Otero car keys. The same day the stuff was discovered, several of the cars of library employees were broken into and personal information was taken out of the glove boxes. She said that every branch of the library is under surveillance by the KBI. The employees whose cars were broken into are being protected.
Very interesting if true. However, none of those details have been confirmed.

Subject: The BTK Diet
Tom, I have some info on BTK. This is stuff law enforcement was told at the time, but has never been made public to my knowledge. After the Otero murders BTK drove the Otero car to a small store on the corner of 9th and Oliver. He bought bananas and bologna. The store owner and the butcher saw him face to face. He had a trench coat on and they also saw the Otero car. They didn't know anything about the murders at the time, but after seeing the news that night they knew they had seen him and they called the police. Police came and took fingerprints and statements. I also know that after BTK dumped the car at Dillions at Central and Oliver, he walked down the 600 block of N. Harding. (Well, someone wearing a trench coat did, anyway.) This was reported to law enforcement, but they didn't follow up on it. I know this doesn't amount to much, but what if someone knows someone who eats bananas and bologna all the time?
Bananas and bologna, eh? And I had always thought the "B" in BTK stood for "bind." But just for the record, police have denied such an incident occurred.
Subject: Jacob Allen
My friend is related to the Sumner County boy who was recently bound and killed on the train tracks and said there was a note left and it was a definite suicide. She also said that one of the detectives says that BTK is trying to take credit; he supposedly had some info about the crime scene.
The police eventually confirmed the death was a suicide, although that ruling has been hard for many to accept. If BTK has claimed responsibility, it's a detail the police have kept secret.
