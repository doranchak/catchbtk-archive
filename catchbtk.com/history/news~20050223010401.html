Feb. 18, 2005 --
Wichita Police Lt. Ken Lanwehr yesterday revealed that FBI behavioral-analysis experts have confirmed communications dropped anonymously last October and December came from BTK. The October communication was a letter containing information about BTK. The December communication, a package, contained a driver's license belonging to BTK victim Nancy Fox, Lanwehr said the FBI is still analyzing three remaining communications. So far, six other BTK communications, many containing items of jewelry, have been confirmed as authentic since the serial killer resurfaced last March with taunts to police and news media after a 30-year hiatus.

In an earlier development, KSAS-TV received a suspicious package Feb. 16. After station employees opened the manila envelope and discovered a necklace, the material was turned over to police. The package had a return address of P.J. Fox. Nancy Fox, whose necklace is still missing, was a BTK victim. KSAS is a Fox affiliate. And BTK is known for playing word games.
